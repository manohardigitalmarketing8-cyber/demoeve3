What We Offer

Production Services

Complete technical production solutions for events of all sizes.

Stage Design & Setup

Custom stage fabrication, modular staging systems, scenic design, multi-level platforms, and branded stage builds for any venue or outdoor setting.

LED Wall Installation

High-resolution P2.5 to P10 LED video walls, indoor and outdoor screens, curved displays, and transparent LED panels for stunning visual impact.

Professional Sound Systems

Line array systems, subwoofers, stage monitors, wireless microphones, and complete audio engineering for crystal-clear sound at any scale.

Concert Lighting

Dynamic lighting rigs with moving heads, LED pars, beam lights, follow spots, strobes, haze machines, and custom lighting design.

Truss & Rigging

Aluminum truss structures, roof systems, rigging for speakers and lights, and safety-certified installations for outdoor and indoor events.

Technical Direction

Experienced technical directors managing all production elements, timelines, cue sheets, and crew coordination for seamless execution.

Event Branding

Large-format printing, vinyl wraps, branded backdrops, fascia branding, standees, banners, and custom scenic elements for brand visibility.

Video Production

Multi-camera video production, IMAG systems, pre-event video content creation, projection mapping, and post-event highlight films.

Live Streaming

Professional live streaming with multi-camera setups, encoding, CDN distribution, and platform integration for hybrid and virtual events.

Drone Coverage

Aerial drone photography and videography for spectacular overhead shots of large events, venue coverage, and cinematic sequences.

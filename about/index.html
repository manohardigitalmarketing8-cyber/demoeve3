Our Story

The Journey of SV Events

SV Events is a premier luxury event management company based in Ravulapalem with over a decade of experience in creating spectacular, one-of-a-kind events. Founded with a passion for creating unforgettable experiences, we have grown into one of India's most trusted names in premium event management.

We specialize in transforming ordinary occasions into extraordinary celebrations that leave lasting impressions. Our team of creative professionals, designers, and event architects work tirelessly to bring your vision to life. From intimate gatherings to grand productions, we handle every detail with precision, creativity, and unmatched professionalism.

We believe every event tells a story. Our mission is to craft that story with elegance, innovation, and a touch of magic—ensuring your special moments become timeless memories that are cherished for generations.

Our Journey

The Story of Two Brands

Our journey began in 2014 with SHR Sounds & Lighting, establishing ourselves as a trusted event production partner. Years of supporting event companies, wedding planners and production houses inspired us to launch SR EVENNTSS, our premium event management brand. Today, both brands continue to operate side by side — SR EVENNTSS delivers complete event management while SHR Sounds & Lighting continues to provide technical production and infrastructure for companies.
